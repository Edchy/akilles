# Onboarding film — script, draft 3

**Humbell** — App Store: "Humbell: Strength Training" (subtitle not chosen yet). The film's last line: *Put your ego down. Pick up the bell.*

Short and sweet: about 90 seconds, about real gym life, where things don't go to plan. It opens the intro and can be skipped, and the intro pages still explain the rest.

**On hold:** the app's screens are still changing, so nothing gets captured or recorded until the UI settles. Only this script is ready.

Each line is one caption, and the voice says it as written. Times are rough.

## Notes

- **Reordering is hold and drag, not a swipe.** A swipe on a workout shows Pause and Delete. The script says it that way so it matches the app.
- **The silent opening** needs real-sounding gym sound, a few seconds long. ElevenLabs can generate sound effects as well as voices, so it can come from the same subscription. The same sound, quieter, can come back under the gym chapter.
- **The opening and the close are a pair.** "You walk into the gym. Now what?" is answered by "You walk into the gym. And you know what to do.", in the same dark and the same sound.
- **"Bell" needs the picture.** On its own, "Pick up the bell" could mean a doorbell to someone who has never trained. The humble + dumbbell merge shows what it means at the moment it's said. Anywhere without that picture (App Store, website), use a plainer line or show the same merge as an image.
- **"An honest six beats a sloppy eight"** says the philosophy without a lecture. The intro pages explain how weights and reps move.
- **Left out to keep it short:** away mode, the Library, the workout editor, ⋯ → Swap, the pull-down overview of a workout, and how progression works. All of these are covered in the intro pages or found later.
- **The holiday scene uses loops** (built 16 November 2026), not pausing.
- **Check before recording:**
  - the default workouts, which are Push, Pull and Legs today;
  - the Bodyweight button's wording;
  - that swiping left and right between cards still works this way.
