# Intro film — script, draft 11 (Klay)

**Klay. Get in shape.** One clay story, told by the body itself: warm, playful, human, not a sales pitch. It's for people who are afraid of the gym because nobody ever showed them what to do. The app appears only lightly (Start, a circle); the hints teach the rest. Voice: `jv41DhCf464zw0TI7I1w` (the user's pick) on `eleven_v4`, recorded in one take. Earlier drafts are in git history.

Pictures are in `art/clay/` (from `scenes.json`); each scene's poses flip like stop-motion. The ending is the user's clay K film, `public/logo/klay-k.mp4` (logo: `art/logo/k-black.png`).

## Notes
- Never say "clay" aloud: it sounds the same as Klay. The picture shows the clay, and the name is said once, at the end.
- No "PPL" and no "push, pull, legs".
- Never "lift less"; "slowly" and "light" mean the weight and the pace, never fewer days.
- Not about progress: it's about consistency, going slow, form and longevity.
- Still to redo: `young_run` 1 (the strides are nearly the same); maybe push `sit` 1 deeper into the cushion.
